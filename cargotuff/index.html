The range

What Cargotuff builds.

01

Truck bodies

Durable, efficient bodies for dry freight, specialised freight and the cold chain, with options to fit your cargo.

  • Rigicore fibreglassRobust double-skin panel construction
  • Single skin fibreglassGeneral cargo, single or double doors, floor finish options
  • Insulated bodies65, 90, 115 or 165 mm PU, XPS or EPS pressed fibreglass panels for chilled, frozen and deep-frozen loads
  • FerrotuffPolyurethane core with vapour-tight metal cover layers
  • Open and closed steelCorrugated steel bodies; tail lifts can be fitted to any body
  • 40 ft bodiesInsulated or dry cargo, for more payload per trip
  • Day-old chick bodiesInsulated and ventilated for live transport
02

Pick-up bodies

Strong, secure bodies that do not add excessive weight to the pick-up, for government fleets, the army, commercial and leisure users.

  • PreciseFits without changing the look of the pick-up
  • GlideMulti-purpose, enclosed or windowed
  • RaveStreamlined to reduce drag
  • Insulated fibreglass canopyFor perishables and temperature-sensitive medical products
  • Steel canopyHigh load-bearing, sliding windows available
  • Also in the rangeRhinotop, Inline, Tufftop, V90, Volume Maker
