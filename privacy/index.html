It should be reviewed against UAE law (including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data) and Kenyan law (Data Protection Act, 2019) before it is relied on.

Who we are

This website, sairaj.global, is operated by Sai Raj Trading FZCO, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, the Dubai arm of Sai Raj Limited, Nairobi, Kenya ("Sai Raj", "we", "us"). Registered address and licence number to be added.

Questions about this notice or your information: info@sairaj.global.

What we collect, and why

  • Enquiry form. When you send an enquiry we receive what you type: your name, company, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, sector, the type of enquiry and your message. We use it to reply to you and to prepare a quotation.
  • Sai Raj Club sign-up. If you join the rewards club we receive your name, email and phone or WhatsApp number, and use them to set up and run your membership.
  • Orders. When you check out you are taken to a checkout operated by Shopify. Shopify collects your contact, delivery and payment details to process the order, and shares the order details with us so that we can fulfil it. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email. If you contact us directly we receive whatever you send, and use it to respond.
  • Your cart. The items in your cart are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there when you come back. This stays on your device and is not sent to us until you check out.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type and time, to keep the site secure and working.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website. Update this sentence if analytics are added later.

Who processes it for us

  • Formspree delivers enquiry and club forms to our email inbox.
  • Shopify operates the checkout and payment for shop orders.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries messages you choose to send us there.

These providers may process information outside the UAE and Kenya, under their own privacy terms. Lawyer to confirm the cross-border transfer wording and any agreements required.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and order records for as long as needed to deal with your request, run our business relationship with you, and meet legal and tax record-keeping duties. Specific retention periods to be confirmed.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email info@sairaj.global and we will respond. You can clear your saved cart at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this notice changes we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
