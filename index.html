Our story

01 / 05

  1. 1975 · Nairobi, Kenya

    It began with fibreglass.

    Sai Raj was founded in Kenya in 1975 — a Nairobi workshop, hand-laying fibreglass.

  2. Manufacturing

    A pioneer of the material.

    The workshop became a manufacturer, and a pioneer of fibreglass in Kenya. Under the Cargotuff name came truck bodies, pick-up bodies and conversions, built to last.

  3. Cold chain

    Then we learned to hold the cold.

    Insulated Cargotuff bodies, cold rooms and Thermo King refrigeration: the infrastructure that keeps a harvest fresh between the farm and the buyer.

  4. Across Africa

    Exported to more than ten countries.

    Horticulture, floriculture, dairy and meat processing businesses across the continent run on equipment built in Kenya. Today we export to 10+ countries in Africa.

  5. Dubai and the Gulf

    Now, the Gulf.

    Sai Raj Trading FZCO is our Dubai arm, bringing five decades of fibreglass and cold-chain experience to the UAE and the wider Gulf.

Sectors we serve

The group

One company. Six brands.

Sai Raj is Kenya’s leading fibreglass moulder, commercial vehicle body manufacturer and vehicle conversion specialist. Each brand carries one part of that work.

  • Ambulances and mobile clinics. Explore Pulse
  • Open game vehicles, 4x4 conversions and steel accessories. Explore Safarituff

What's next

Heritage, meeting technology.

Fifty years of making the box. The next chapter is making it intelligent, and making it run on the sun.

  • On the roadmap

    Smart cold-chain monitoring

    Temperature and location, reported live from the body to the buyer, so a break in the chain is seen before it costs a shipment.

  • On the roadmap

    Composites with sensors

    Fibreglass panels that report on themselves, with sensing built into the laminate.

  • On the roadmap

    Solar cold storage

    Cold storage powered by the sun, aimed at UAE food security and lower energy costs.
